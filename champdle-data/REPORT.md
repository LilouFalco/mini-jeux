Verified on 2026-10-17T18:47:30+02:00. champions.json is a plain array of 173 records for the current League of Legends PC roster in Data Dragon 16.20.1. There are no missing champions, no extra champions, no duplicates, no omitted records, and no empty required fields. The fields are id, name, gender, positions, species, resource, range, regions, and integer year. String ids are Riot's canonical champion ids, such as MonkeyKing for Wukong and Nunu for Nunu & Willump.

The live version list was fetched first from https://ddragon.leagueoflegends.com/api/versions.json, followed by the complete roster from https://ddragon.leagueoflegends.com/cdn/16.20.1/data/en_US/champion.json. Data Dragon supplies every id, numeric key, display name, resource string, class tag, and base attack-range statistic in ddragon.json. The authoritative roster contains Locke, released in 2026, as well as Zaahen, Yunara, and Mel. The source list was checked on the actual current date rather than assuming a 2025 roster.

The complete Meraki document was read from https://cdn.merakianalytics.com/riot/lol/resources/latest/en-US/champions.json, including all continuation chunks. It contains 171 champions and references Data Dragon 15.15.1 artwork. It is stale relative to the current roster and lacks Locke and Zaahen. Relevant source fields are preserved in meraki-fields.json; the 13 MB of unrelated skins and abilities were not bundled. Meraki does not provide species or gender and its faction is a single-valued older affiliation, not a complete list of current lore regions.

The full gameplay Lua table at https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Module:ChampionData/data was read and its current revision is https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Module:ChampionData/data?oldid=4085876, last edited 16 October 2026. It contains 173 integer-id champions plus two fractional-id form records. Fractional ids 150.2 and 240.1 are excluded from the identity join, preventing Mega Gnar from overwriting Gnar. No Lua source was executed. The complete rendered Lua source and extracted fields are preserved locally. The wiki table supplies all 173 release dates, descriptive resources, range types, and client positions.

All 171 overlapping Meraki champions were cross-checked against the wiki, not just a sample. Release dates agree for all 171, and standard-form range types agree for all 171. There are 40 position-set discrepancies and one descriptive-resource discrepancy, Rek'Sai Rage versus Fury. Current wiki client_positions wins for the game; external_positions is preserved separately as nonbinding contextual data. The position data is an intended/client grouping, not a claim about present ranked pick rates. Every difference and its resolution appears in discrepancies.json.

All 173 champions were checked against current wiki Universe lore infoboxes for Species, Pronoun(s), and Region(s), and Willump was additionally checked separately. The complete relevant infobox passages and exact URLs are retained in wiki-infoboxes.json; per-record extracted evidence and release dates are in provenance.json. Only current, non-struck-through species and regional affiliations are used. Historical birthplace, former species, previous affiliations, residence, and faction are not silently merged into Region(s). This is why current Aatrox is Runeterra rather than an inferred Shurima association and why Gwen is Human rather than a former Doll. These are present wiki classifications, not a claim that this game's answers exactly match the independently maintained LoLdle database.

Species strings are normalized conservatively. Parenthetical character labels and traits such as Iceborn or formerly Human are not separate species. Spirit Gods is singularized to Spirit God; Chemical Beast and Evolved Being use consistent title case. God-Warrior is normalized to Ascended based on the source's explicitly listed name and parent species. Vesani, Bryni, Marai, Shimon, Kiilash, Oovi-Kat, and Lhotlan are normalized to their verified parent Vastaya; the fetched taxonomy evidence and URLs appear in taxonomy-evidence.json. Other precise wiki species such as Celestial Dragon, Terrestrial Dragon, Blood Golem, and Wraith are retained without inventing broader taxonomic relationships.

Character scope is explicit. Nunu & Willump combines Human and Yeti because both are named in the actual Riot champion name and each has a separately checked lore infobox. Aphelios excludes his supporting sister Alune, Kled excludes his supporting mount Skaarl, and Nilah excludes her separate bound demon Ashlesh. Kayn's Human and Darkin pairing and Varus's Darkin and Human pairing are retained from their combined current lore infoboxes. Naafiri retains Darkin and Dune Hounds. This scope is a documented game convention, not an attempt to claim the omitted supporting entities do not exist.

The wiki now exposes Pronoun(s), not an independently verified gender field. The required scalar gender is therefore a documented GAME GROUPING, not a factual claim of sex or gender identity. It maps the champion's current She forms to Female, He forms to Male, and It/They-only or mixed Kindred forms to Other. Other never means a specifically confirmed nonbinary identity. Kindred is grouped Other; Aphelios, Kled, and the male constituents of Varus are grouped Male. Neeko uses her female base glamour, Evelynn ignores former It pronouns, Zyra's She/It maps Female, and Tahm Kench's He/They maps Male. The exact source pronouns remain available per champion in provenance.json. If the UI needs to avoid this simplifying convention, label the column as a pronoun grouping or display the preserved pronouns instead of claiming a verified gender identity.

Jax, Rammus, Shaco, and Zyra have an explicitly sourced species value of Unknown. Those values are retained as the literal stated uncertainty; possible Sand-Troll, Baccai, or new-species speculation is NOT promoted to fact. No champion genuinely lacks an entire source field, so no record had to be excluded. Unknown is a meaningful source value rather than an empty placeholder. A stricter mode requiring identified species can exclude those four from secret selection while keeping them available as guesses.

Data Dragon resource strings differ from wiki descriptions for Bel'Veth, Briar, Dr. Mundo, Rek'Sai, and Zac. The resolved values are Manaless, Frenzy, Health, Fury, and Health respectively. Wiki None is normalized to Manaless. Specialized meter names such as Blood Well, Courage, Grit, and Shield are kept as sourced and are not all collapsed into Manaless. See discrepancies.json for the exact raw source pairs.

A numeric base attack range cannot reliably determine melee status. Gnar has a displayed base range of 175 but is Ranged in his normal form, Jayce has a Data Dragon base range of 125 but the wiki and Meraki group him Ranged, and Lillia has range 325 but is Melee. The scalar range uses the source's normal-form classification, not a fabricated greater-than-300 rule. Champions can change form or temporarily change attack range during play; this scalar is intentionally not an exhaustive list of combat states.
